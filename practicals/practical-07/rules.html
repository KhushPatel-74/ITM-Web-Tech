League Rules

Please Read Before Playing

  1. Each team plays with 11 players on the field and at most 5 substitutes.
  2. A match lasts 60 minutes, played as two halves of 30 minutes.
  3. A win gives 3 points, a draw gives 1 point and a loss gives 0 points.
  4. Players must wear shin guards and the team jersey in every match.
  5. Two yellow cards or one red card means the player leaves the match.
  6. The referee's decision is final.
