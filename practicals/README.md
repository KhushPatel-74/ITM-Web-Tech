## Notes on practical 7

The soccer site is built with two frames. `index.html` is the page that holds the frames, `header.html` is the top frame, and `home.html`, `rules.html`, `schedule.html` and `standings.html` are the pages that load in the content frame. Styling is in `soccer.css`. Open `index.html` to see the whole site.
