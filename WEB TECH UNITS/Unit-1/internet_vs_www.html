Introduction to World Wide Web

System of interconnected pages

The World Wide Web (WWW), often called the Web, is a system of interconnected web pages and information that you can access using the Internet. It was created to help people share and find information easily, using links that connect different pages together.

Key Characteristics

Global Interaction:Allows users to browse websites, watch videos, shop online, and connect with others worldwide.

The W3 Pool:All public websites accessible through the internet on any local device are collectively known as W3.

Hyperlink Navigation: Users discover further information by navigating interlinked pages and documents.

Rich Formats: Information is represented in diverse text, picture, audio, or video formats.



Summary

WWW is a system of interconnected web pages and information accessed through the Internet.
It helps people share, find, and access information using hyperlinks.
Global interaction: Users can browse websites, watch videos, shop, and communicate wordlwide.
Hyperlink navigation: Links connect different web pages and documents.
Rich formats: Information can include text, images, audio, and video.
W3: Refers to the collection of publicly accessible websites on the Internet.
Global connectivity: The Web connects over 63% of the world's population.
