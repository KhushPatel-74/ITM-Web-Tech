WHAT IS A FOOD DRIVE COMMUNITY?

A Food Drive community is a Charity driven communnity with a dedicated team of people who collect, sort and donate food items for families and people in need.

Collection:

Goods like rice, healthy snacks, oatmeals, etc are collected from neighbourhood, local schools or community centres.

Support:

In partnership with NGO and large network like FEEDING INDIA to drop off supplies

KEY GOALS

Providing steady meals for people who lack access to daily food.

Raising Awareness about local poverty and food shortages.

To bring UNITY among people



Why I joined this community?

I joined this community because i think everyone deserves access to nutritious and healthy food. i wanted to become part of something that could make a real difference in my community. By joining this initiative, i can contribute my time and effort to collect, organize and distribute food to people who need it.

How can you help?

You dont need to make a huge contribution to make a difference, Every small effort counts. You can support ou food drive by:

Donating rice, grains, cereals, healthy snacks and other food items.

Helping us collect and organize donated food.

Volunteering your time during collection and distributing drives.

Encouraging others to become part of this initiative.
