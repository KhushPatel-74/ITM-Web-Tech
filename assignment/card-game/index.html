Memory Match

Flip the cards, remember where they are and find all 8 pairs in the fewest moves.

Start Game

How to play

  1. Click a card to flip it.
  2. Click a second card to look for its pair.
  3. Same emoji? The pair stays open.
  4. Different? Both cards flip back, so remember them.
  5. Match all 8 pairs to win.
